Prohibited Manufacturers

Certain products are not allowed on GSAAdvantage! because they pose a national security risk and are prohibited by law, regulation or other bindings directive. Contractor SIP or EDI catalog submissions will be rejected if a product lists one of the following entities in the manufacturer field:

  • Kaspersky Lab
  • Huawei Technologies Company
  • ZTE Corporation
  • Hytera Communications Corporation
  • Hangzhou Hikvision Digital Technology Company
  • Dahua Technology Company
